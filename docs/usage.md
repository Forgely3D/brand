# Brand Usage Rules

Brand assets in this repository are © Forgely Inc.

## Approved use

You may use the Forgely logo and brand colors **without prior approval** for:

- Reviews and editorial coverage of Forgely products
- Linking to forgely3d.com
- Open-source projects that integrate with Forgely products (no implied endorsement)

## Requires written approval

Email **press@forgely3d.com** before using brand assets for:

- Co-marketing or partnership announcements
- Resale, distribution, or print runs of physical materials
- Modified logo variants
- Any use that could imply endorsement by Forgely Inc.

## Always

- Maintain the clear-space rule (see [`../BRAND.md`](../BRAND.md))
- Use the unmodified logo from [`../logo/`](../logo/)
- Pair the orange gradient logo with light or photographic backgrounds; use the white monochrome variant on dark surfaces

## Never

- Recolor or restyle the logo
- Stretch, skew, or rotate
- Add drop shadows, glows, or strokes
- Combine the Forgely mark with another logo into a single lockup
- Use the brand to imply Forgely's endorsement of a third-party product

For questions: **press@forgely3d.com**
