# Video / Avatar Guidelines

For HeyGen avatars and any on-camera or narrated Forgely content.

| Aspect | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Avatar style** | Professional, confident, approachable |
| **Background** | Clean, modern, minimal — white, light gray, or a subtle brand color |
| **Lighting** | Bright, professional, well-lit |
| **Tone** | Conversational yet authoritative. Direct and energetic. |
| **Pacing** | Moderate speed with natural pauses for emphasis |
| **Closing** | Always end on a call-to-action or brand statement |

Copy in video scripts is still subject to [`copy-compliance.md`](copy-compliance.md) —
same forbidden phrases, topic limits, and competitor rules apply.
