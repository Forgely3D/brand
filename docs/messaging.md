## Core message

> Premium 3D printing filament engineered in the USA with ±0.02mm precision.

## Messaging pillars

- **Why Forgely** — Engineered consistency. Trusted quality. Made in Utah.
- **Positioning** — "Made in Ogden, Utah — not imported."
- **Unique selling point** — Precision manufacturing at scale. Same-day shipping from Utah.

## Who we serve

Makers, print farms, manufacturers, businesses, and hobbyists — from classrooms and
makerspaces to engineering prototyping and end-use manufacturing.

## Proof points (use specifics, not adjectives)

- ±0.02mm tolerance standard
- Manufactured in Ogden, Utah — not rebranded imports
- Same-day / fast domestic shipping from Utah
- Free shipping over $49
- PLA from $21.99/kg
- Trusted by print farms
- Real-maker support

## Aesthetic & personality

- **Aesthetic:** Modern, clean, engineering-forward
- **Personality:** Professional yet approachable. Confident. Precise. Makers-minded.
- **Tone:** Direct, honest, action-oriented. We solve problems. We deliver quality.
